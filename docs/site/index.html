Runner

Runner is a free, simple endless runner for iPhone and iPad. A little runner dashes down three railway lanes. Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Dodge hurdles, duck under overhead bars, steer around trains and grab as many coins as you can. One hit ends the run, and your best score is saved on your device.
